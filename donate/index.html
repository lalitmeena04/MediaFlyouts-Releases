Questions & answers

QDo I get anything for donating?
ANo. A donation is a thank-you, nothing more. Pro features are a separate one-time purchase in the Microsoft Store app, and the installer and portable builds already include everything.
QIs it safe to pay to a UPI ID from a web page?
AYes. Paying to a UPI ID is a push payment: only you can start it, from your own app, with your own PIN. Never approve an incoming request for money you did not initiate, and check that the payee name your app shows matches the one on this page.
QWhich amount should I give?
AWhatever you like. The quick amounts are only suggestions; scanning the code without an amount lets you type one in your UPI app.
QI paid by mistake. Can I get a refund?
AYes. Open an issue with the date and the last four digits of the transaction reference. The refund goes back to the account the payment came from.
QCan I donate from outside India?
AUPI and NEFT are domestic Indian payment rails. From abroad, the tips in the Microsoft Store version are the simplest way, and they are charged in your local currency.
QDoes this page track me?
ANo. It has no analytics, no cookies and no third-party scripts. The QR code is drawn in your browser from the details shown above, and the Copy buttons only write to your clipboard.
